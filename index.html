Help & Install

A quick guide to exploring the Ridge Trail, tracking your progress, and saving the Passport to your phone.

Using the Passport

Explore the Ridge Trail. Use the map or section list to browse the trail. Tap a main Ridge Trail line or a section in the list to see details.

Track your progress. When you complete a main Ridge Trail section, tap Mark this section complete. Your completed sections, mileage, and overall progress update automatically.

Find sections. Use search, County and Region filters, or turn on Uncompleted only to focus on sections you still have left.

Use your location. Tap the location button on the map to zoom to your current location. Your browser may ask for permission first.

Your progress is saved on this device and browser. It does not currently sync between devices, and clearing browser or site data can erase it.

Add the Passport to your Home Screen

Save the Passport to your phone so it opens more like a regular app.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Passport in Safari.
  2. Tap the ••• menu in the bottom-right.
  3. Tap the Share button.
  4. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  5. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open the Passport in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the upper-right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Follow the prompts to install it.

The exact wording may vary slightly depending on your phone and browser.

Offline Use

After you open the Passport online, the app and latest Ridge Trail route information are saved for offline use. Your completion history also remains available offline.

The OpenStreetMap background map still requires an internet connection, so the basemap may be blank or incomplete when you are offline.

About This Beta

The Ridge Trail Passport is still in beta. Trail information may change, and the Passport should not be your only source for navigation or current trail conditions.
